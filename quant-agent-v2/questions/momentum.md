# Momentum research question

Under what market conditions does cross-sectional momentum in liquid U.S. equities
remain robust after realistic transaction costs, and which observable regime filters
improve out-of-sample risk-adjusted performance without introducing look-ahead bias?

Define the universe, signal formation and holding periods, rebalance schedule,
weighting, benchmark, cost model, turnover constraints, risk controls, and evaluation
periods. Require walk-forward or otherwise genuinely out-of-sample validation. Treat
all numerical conclusions as unverified until reproduced from cited data.
